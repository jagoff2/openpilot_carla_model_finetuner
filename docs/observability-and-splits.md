**Observability and split admission**

The current corpus configuration uses the stock MED and SBIG crops and the CD210/C210M training contract. Physical camera optics are nominal assumptions, not calibration to a real comma device. [Preprocessing](../lane_dataset/preprocessing.py) binds the existing exact warp arithmetic, deterministic synthetic RGB-to-NV12 conversion, six-plane packing, and native image bytes. [Visibility](../lane_dataset/visibility.py) samples luma and aligned depth with that same warp. Precomputed canonical source luma may be reused to avoid a second full-resolution RGB conversion; it must match the source frame dimensions and byte type.

Each boundary-point claim requires positive camera depth, independently qualified depth convention/alignment, measured depth agreement, and RGB contrast in sampled pixels. The approved boundary contract includes painted lane markings, visible curbs, and road edges. An edge requires an adjacent road-to-sidewalk, terrain, or ground transition, at least 12 luma contrast, and depth agreement on both sides. Semantic labels alone cannot grant support. Frustum inclusion alone cannot qualify a point. The processed crop is tested independently of the uncropped source image, and edge clamping cannot turn an out-of-crop geometric projection into support. Paint and edge detector identities are retained separately. Yellow paint on bright asphalt can qualify through at least 6 units of U/V color contrast in the exact stock NV12 chroma planes, with a RoadLines semantic sample, luma of at least 70, and matching depth at the same pixel. Admission replays the U/V contrast in the authenticated packed frame; no extra model channel is introduced. Edge evidence retains its 9x9 semantic masks so admission replays contrast in the authenticated stock-packed RGB luma. Representative rendered review and rendered-boundary validation remain necessary.

Physical road elevation is measured by collision rays, independent of camera depth. Curb boundaries retain the original lane XY and use road-level toe elevation extrapolated from road hits 20 and 40 cm inward. Independently raycast interior points qualify the depth convention. Rendered paint centers and RGB-contrasted road edges are measured in 5 mm cross-sections and must still agree with lane geometry within 5 cm. Lateral acceleration (3.5 m/s²), lateral jerk (5 m/s³), body clearance, and centering limits are unchanged. Depth and semantic images are QA signals, never additional student inputs.

Evidence references contain simulator frame ID, simulation timestamp, and camera identity. The history ledger rejects duplicate camera/frame entries and timestamp disorder, requires both current stock-camera frames for a decision, excludes newer frames when querying an older decision, and expires evidence after 4.8 seconds. That interval bounds admitted observations. It does not prove that a student's recurrent state retained a cue or can use it for control.

| Visibility category | Meaning | Release counting |
|---|---|---|
| `current` | Required geometry has current stock-crop evidence | May qualify after all other gates |
| `history_supported` | Required geometry relies on earlier admitted crop evidence | Does not automatically satisfy the crop-loss quota |
| `history_crop_loss` | Required preview is missing from current crops, visible in the current uncropped source, and supported by earlier crop pixels | Counts only after independent ledger checks |
| `diagnostic_ambiguous` | Required or immediate-action geometry lacks admitted evidence | Excluded from accepted-label counts |

The crop-loss quota check requires at least 0.10 s of measured prior visibility and at least 2 m of useful preview loss relative to the current crop preview. Duration sums adjacent visible 20 Hz intervals; it does not bridge missing observations. The compact ledger stores required-point masks, current crop/source masks, distances, evidence frames, and measured durations. A category string or the nominal recurrent-buffer length cannot fill the quota. Historical support where the uncropped source is also occluded remains outside this conservative crop-specific quota.

[Admission replay](../lane_dataset/admission.py) resolves evidence IDs and timestamps against authenticated retained frames, verifies the packed archive bindings and projected geometry, and checks marking contrast in actual packed pixels. It rebuilds the history sequentially instead of trusting the candidate's support flags. Missing, duplicate, corrupted, or temporally inconsistent evidence cannot lend support to earlier labels. Depth conventions and compact source/depth claims also depend on the recorded fixture qualification and capture implementation; the full source RGB/depth streams are not retained at every timestamp. Selected full-resolution audit frames and the hashed compact ledger support review, with that storage limitation documented.

Controller references are reconstructed from admitted paired boundary knots. The current capture/admission path can interpolate across a bounded marking gap, using only admitted knots and their observed chord coordinates. It does not slice a spline fitted against hidden map points. The independent admission check compares the reconstructed intervals with physical map geometry, masks intervals exceeding the allowed geometric disagreement, and rejects immediate actions that require extrapolation outside the observed corridor. Map agreement validates a hypothesis; it does not create new image evidence. This interpolation policy still requires rendered development qualification on dashed markings, bends, intersections, and occlusions.

Plan labels use the actual recorded physical trajectory on the 33-point time grid. Each interval must have a continuous supported path, including the space between adjacent target times. Once an unsupported interval occurs, the remaining future segments are masked. Per-element plan masks and the lateral-action mask remain independent: a valid immediate curvature command need not imply a valid ten-second plan, and unsupported plan values contribute no supervised loss. Lateral raw output 2062 uses `max(1,current_speed)^2 × commanded_curvature`; no longitudinal target is fabricated. Unrelated and uncertainty heads remain unsupervised. The temporal loader rebuilds student recurrence from dense retained frames rather than loading frozen teacher features.

Physical warmup currently precedes retained recording. Unretained initialization observations cannot be used as training evidence. Admission replays only retained frames, and the required five-second retained prefix must be preserved before the first label. Intersection raw desire alone is insufficient: a correctly timed rising-edge pulse must be present before the decision that requires route intent. Startup pulses that have expired from the supported desire window do not qualify later turns.

[Split grouping](../lane_dataset/splits.py) namespaces native road and junction identifiers by town. Road intervals normalize traversal direction and omit lane sign, so reverse and overlapping lane traversals stay together. Shared junction IDs and directly joined junction-road objects form a common group. Ordinary roads between separate intersections are handled through their recorded intervals; arbitrary city-wide reachability is not treated as one junction object. This topology policy must be checked against the rendered observation envelopes before a split is frozen.

Procedural grouping uses canonical physical shape parameters, excluding mirror-turn and split annotations. A scenario-family label cannot make identical procedural parameters independent. The generated radius lattices differ across splits, and mirrored appearances remain within one group. Explicit geometry identities and appearance variants are grouped before assignment. Native Town05 sections are reserved for test, including groups that also contain procedural metadata. The final overlap audit regroups raw sections, so altered group names alone cannot conceal reuse.

The current [planner validation report](../reports/scenario-planner-validation.md) describes 150 structurally selected groups and 250 planned tasks. It documents zero accepted shards at its audit timestamp. The **90 m native observation envelope remains unqualified**: its 25 m preview assumption does not bound everything visible in source images or stock crops, and the original distance calculation omits physical rolling warmup. Visibility probes cover only the planned route, so they cannot prove that other rendered roads are absent. Measure the actual retained observation footprint, expand and regroup all affected roads/junctions, and replan if needed before freezing configuration or publishing training shards.

Release accounting still requires exactly 4,000/500/500 accepted decisions, five equally represented families, the procedural cap, at least 25% independently supported crop-loss labels per split, complete sequence/hash checks, expert-quality gates, and a representative continuous clip review for every group. [The gallery](../lane_dataset/gallery.py) only presents actual recorded audit imagery and starts every review pending. Review dispositions bind the manifest, audit-image, and clip hashes; generating the gallery never marks geometry qualified. Development renders and unit tests do not establish full-corpus visual or physical qualification.

Double centerlines use the stripe facing the driven lane as the painted boundary. CARLA places each stripe center one declared marking width from the unpainted OpenDRIVE divider. The reference retains all original coordinates and marking metadata for audit. Separate rendered checks retain the same 5 cm limit. The lane center is the midpoint of its two selected physical boundaries.

Depth agreement is evaluated at the exact sampled pixel, including the stock nearest-neighbor warp. A local road-surface tangent plane predicts the depth at that pixel; comparing it to the unrounded reference point would falsely reject valid distant paint when a one-pixel row difference represents substantial longitudinal distance. This geometric prediction is only a depth/occlusion check. Controller geometry still comes exclusively from admitted boundary knots, and depth tolerance is unchanged.

Physical spawn initialization may use an explicitly tagged map-based driver before retained recording. These controls are never accepted expert commands and cannot generate labels. Readiness still requires a steady interval of accepted pixel-only MPC control, calibrated speed, and physical jerk. Every retained frame uses the pixel-only controller, and the first label remains after five seconds of retained conditioning. A map initializer raises if invoked during recording.
